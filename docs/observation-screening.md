## Policy

- Journal whitelist: **TGRS, ISPRS JPRS, RSE, TPAMI, TIP, IJCV, TMM**. The criterion follows the requested top-journal tier; it does not equate every JCR Q1 or CAS first-quartile journal with the core.
- Conference whitelist: **CVPR, ICCV, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AAAI, ACM MM**, using the 2026 CCF directory and main-conference full/regular papers. Workshops, Findings and submission status do not establish core acceptance. [CCF directory](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/By_category/).
- Other high-level journals and historical ranking differences remain explicitly pending. IJCAI 2022 was A at publication but is B in the 2026 directory. IGARSS is retained as supplementary rather than treated as an ordinary low-quality EI conference.
- Formal publication is supported by DOI title checks or official CVF, PMLR and ICLR publication pages. Preserve preprint identity when a formal version changes its title or venue.
- This is a venue/publication filter, **not a new per-paper full-text audit**. Existing primary subclasses are retained. LST, 3D and non-generative reconstruction boundaries still need future content review.
- The five recently identified additions remain candidates outside this 206-record cohort and are not promoted by this publishing step. No raw workbook, manuscript PDF, internal notes or local paths are published.
